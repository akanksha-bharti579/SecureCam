# scfp1 robustness experiment on real smartphone photos

Generated by `tests/experiment/vision` (make_vision.py, analyze_vision.js). Photos: 1000 from 35 device(s) (174 stored rotated with an EXIF orientation flag). Copies scored: 33000. Fingerprints and all scoring use securecam-core.js (thumbnails re-checked on 5 images).

Split per device: calibration 315 photos (10395 copies), test 685 photos (22605 copies).

Photo sizes: 0.7 to 20.7 megapixels; aspect ratios: 1.33, 1.34, 1.77, 1.78.

## Same file, phone decode vs full decode

Max pHash difference 2 bits, max region difference luma 7.96 / chroma 1.54.

## Distinct photos

499500 pairs of different photos. pHash distance: minimum 4 bits; pairs within the lookup threshold (at most 12 bits): 12.

## Shipped defaults on all photos

lookup at most 12 bits or 2 quadrants within 6; region luma 9, chroma 7. These values were fixed on the earlier 22-image experiment, before this dataset was used, so all 1000 photos are unseen data for them.

- Unedited copies recognised as authentic copies: **12996/13000 (99.97%; 95% CI 99.9-100.0%)**
- False accepts (a copy called an authentic copy of a different photo): **0**
- Visible edits rejected (MODIFIED or UNVERIFIED): **14821/17406 (85.1%; 95% CI 84.6-85.7%)**; shown as MODIFIED: 14805, heatmap covering the edit: 14799

Test split only:

- Unedited copies recognised as authentic copies: **8904/8905 (99.99%; 95% CI 99.9-100.0%)**
- False accepts (a copy called an authentic copy of a different photo): **0**
- Visible edits rejected (MODIFIED or UNVERIFIED): **10132/11928 (84.9%; 95% CI 84.3-85.6%)**; shown as MODIFIED: 10124, heatmap covering the edit: 10120
