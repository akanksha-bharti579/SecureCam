# scfp1 robustness experiment on real smartphone photos

Generated by `tests/experiment/vision` (make_vision.py, analyze_vision.js). Photos: 204 from 1 device(s) (204 stored rotated with an EXIF orientation flag). Copies scored: 6120. Fingerprints and all scoring use securecam-core.js (thumbnails re-checked on 5 images).

Split per device: calibration 63 photos (1890 copies), test 141 photos (4230 copies).

Photo sizes: 12.5 to 12.5 megapixels; aspect ratios: 1.33.

## Same file, phone decode vs full decode

Max pHash difference 2 bits, max region difference luma 8.72 / chroma 1.26.

## Distinct photos

20706 pairs of different photos. pHash distance: minimum 0 bits; pairs within the lookup threshold (at most 12 bits): 78.

## Shipped defaults on all photos

lookup at most 12 bits or 2 quadrants within 6; region luma 9, chroma 7. These values were fixed on the earlier 22-image experiment, before this dataset was used, so all 204 photos are unseen data for them.
